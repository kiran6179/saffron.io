WELCOME TO SAFFRON & STONE

Rooted in tradition.
Made for today.

We believe the best dining experiences are built on simple things: honest ingredients, generous flavours, and people who care.

Inspired by India's rich culinary heritage, our kitchen brings regional favourites and contemporary techniques together in a relaxed, considered setting. Whether it is a quick lunch or a long dinner with friends, there is always room at our table.
